1.Who we are and what this policy covers

Craft Funnel Services ("we", "us") designs websites and sales funnels for small businesses, coaches and freelancers. This policy explains what personal information we collect on craftfunnelservices.com, in our free audit and discovery form, and when we work with you on a project. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

Personal information controller: Edralyn Cabungcag, Craft Funnel Services, Bacolod City, Western Visayas, Philippines.

Contact for privacy questions: hello@craftfunnelservices.com

Effective date: October 17, 2026

2.What information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and do the work.

  • Sign-up and contact forms. When you request a free audit, a quote, or to start a project, we collect your name and email address.
  • Free audit discovery form. Our Google Form asks about your business: its name, what you offer, your ideal customer, your website and platform, social media links, how you collect leads, your goals and challenges, your timeline, an optional budget range, and how you prefer to be reached (including a phone or messaging number if you choose to give one).
  • Emails and messages. The messages you send us and our replies, including any files or links you share.
  • Client project material. When you hire us, we may receive your logo, photos, product details, brand colors, copy, and login or access details to your domain, hosting, email or funnel tools so we can build and publish your work.
  • Technical data. Our web host and the services below may log your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you visit.

We do not ask for government ID numbers, card numbers or other sensitive personal information. Please do not include them in the form or in emails. Payments, if any, are handled by the payment method we agree on with you.

4.Who we share it with

We use trusted services to run our business. They process your information only to provide their service, under their own privacy terms.

  • Google (Gmail, Google Forms, Google Drive, Google Sheets): receives your emails and your discovery form answers, which are stored in our Google account. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address.
  • Website hosting and domain providers (for example Hostinger or Netlify): serve our website and may keep standard server logs.
  • Form delivery service (FormSubmit): passes the name and email you type into our website forms to our inbox.
  • Email follow-up tools (for example Systeme.io): may store your name and email and send you the emails you asked for, if we use them to deliver your audit or follow-ups.
  • Facebook: if you follow or message us there, Facebook's own privacy policy applies.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights. If our business is ever sold or reorganized, your information may transfer to the new owner under this same policy.

5.Client websites and funnels

When we build a website or funnel for you, you decide what personal information it collects from your own visitors, such as leads, orders and email subscribers. For that data, you are the controller and we act only on your instructions to set it up and maintain it.

You are responsible for your own privacy policy, consent notices and legal compliance on your site. We can help you add them, but we do not provide legal advice.

We keep the access details you give us (logins, hosting and domain access) only as long as the project needs them, and we ask that you change those passwords when we finish.

6.Cookies and tracking

Our website does not use advertising cookies or analytics tracking of its own. Services we rely on, such as Google Fonts, embedded forms and our hosting provider, may set technical cookies or record visits under their own policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

If we add analytics or advertising tools later, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

7.Retention, security and transfers

How long we keep it. We keep your form answers and emails while we are in contact and for up to 2 years after our last communication, so we can follow up on your request. We keep contracts and invoices for as long as Philippine tax and accounting rules require. After that, we delete or anonymize them.

How we protect it. We use two-step verification on our email and Google account, password-protected access to our tools, and we share information only with the services listed above. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Transfers abroad. Google, Systeme.io, FormSubmit and other tools may store data on servers outside the Philippines. By using our forms you understand your information may be processed there under those providers' safeguards.

If something goes wrong. If a breach affects your personal information in a way that creates serious risk, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

8.Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your information is used, which this policy does;
  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • object to our processing, or withdraw your consent at any time;
  • ask us to delete or block your information when it is no longer needed, or if it is unlawfully processed;
  • receive a copy of your information in a common format (data portability);
  • claim compensation for damages caused by inaccurate, unlawful or unauthorized use of your data; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, email hello@craftfunnelservices.com from the address you used with us. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, and we will reply within a reasonable time. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.

9.Children, changes and contact

Children. Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes. We may update this policy as our services or the law change. The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and we will post updates on this page.
